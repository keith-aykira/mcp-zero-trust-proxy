## Recommendation

After a rigorous evaluation of 49 side business opportunities across 13 weighted dimensions and 10 independent research reports — spanning market trigger strength, pain severity, revenue potential, competitive density, pricing power, buildability, time-to-revenue, revenue model durability, market size, defensibility, geographic flexibility, and founder fit — we recommend pursuing **MCP Zero-Trust Proxy** as the primary venture. Scoring 8.1 composite (the highest of all 49 ideas, sourced from R9), it addresses the critical security gap in the rapidly expanding MCP ecosystem: no turnkey solution exists to enforce authentication, rate limiting, and audit logging on MCP servers that are currently exposed without access controls. The $1.8B TAM (MCP/API security), combined with a tiered pricing model ($49-$299/mo), creates compounding revenue as MCP adoption accelerates. With a 10-14 day MVP timeline and your existing infrastructure experience, this is the fastest high-value path to revenue. Close behind, **AI Cost Attribution** (8.0, R7) remains the strongest billing-layer play, capitalizing on Stripe's March 2, 2026 launch of AI cost-tracking features with a $200M SAM and freemium model ($99-$299/mo plus 0.5% transaction fees). For maximum upside, we recommend a parallel three-product play: launch **MCP Zero-Trust Proxy** in weeks 1-3, then **Safer Auto Mode** (7.5, R10 — trust layer for agentic coding tools, 10-14 day MVP, $29-$299/mo) in weeks 2-5, followed by **AI Cost Attribution** in weeks 4-10. These three products form a cohesive "AI agent infrastructure" platform — security, trust, and billing — serving the same buyer (AI-native developers), sharing technical infrastructure, and compounding into a platform narrative. The synthesis now includes 15 A-tier ideas (up from 13), with two new entrants from R10: Safer Auto Mode (7.5) and Plugin & Skill Ops (7.3). StockPilot and FBA Prep Compliance (both 7.7) remain excellent lower-risk alternatives with forced migration deadlines and marketplace-native distribution, and should be the fallback if Week 1 validation of the AI infrastructure thesis fails to find 10+ developers with active security or billing pain.
