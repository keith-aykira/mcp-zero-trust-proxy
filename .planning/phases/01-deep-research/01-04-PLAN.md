Read ALL 5 research reports produced by Plans 01-03, plus the baseline ATTACK-SURFACE.md. Synthesize into a single decision brief.

**Process:**

1. Read each report's "Kill Criteria Assessment" section.
2. Cross-reference findings across reports (e.g., competitor pricing from COMPETITIVE-ANALYSIS.md vs buyer willingness from BUYER-BEHAVIOR.md).
3. Score each kill criterion on a 3-level scale:
   - **PASS** (green): Evidence clearly supports proceeding
   - **CAUTION** (yellow): Mixed signals, proceed with awareness
   - **FAIL** (red): Evidence suggests killing or pivoting

4. Make an overall recommendation:
   - **GO**: 0 FAIL criteria, 0-2 CAUTION criteria
   - **CONDITIONAL GO**: 0 FAIL criteria, 3+ CAUTION criteria (proceed with mitigations)
   - **PIVOT**: 1 FAIL criterion that could be addressed by changing approach
   - **KILL**: 2+ FAIL criteria, or 1 FAIL on "pain is theoretical"

**Critical guidance:** Be intellectually honest. If the evidence is weak, say so. If a kill criterion fails, recommend killing or pivoting -- do not rationalize. Andrew needs truth, not optimism. The goal is to make the best decision, not to justify proceeding.

For confidence ratings: HIGH means multiple independent sources agree. MEDIUM means some evidence but gaps. LOW means relying on inference or single sources.
